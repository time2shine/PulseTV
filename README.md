# PulseTV

> **High-Performance Android TV & Mobile Streaming Application**  
> Developed by **Muhammad Rokonujjaman**

PulseTV is a completely free, ad-free IPTV streaming platform built for Android TV, Google TV, Amazon Fire TV / Fire Stick, and Android phones & tablets (Android 7.0+). Designed from the ground up with zero manual setup — install and start watching immediately with low-latency HLS/TS playback, an interactive Bangladesh Standard Time (BST) TV Guide, and zero-touch Trakt synchronization.

---

## Key Features

- **1,600+ Live TV Channels (14 Categories)**:
  - Includes 93+ Bangla channels, 27+ Bangla News channels, Indian, International News, Movies, Music, Kids, and Education channels.
  - Fast channel zapping, Favorites pinning, and Recent channels history.
- **Dedicated Live Sports Hub**:
  - Live schedules for Football (UEFA Champions League, La Liga, Serie A, etc.), Cricket, Basketball, Motorsport, and more.
  - `On Now`, `Today`, and `Upcoming` filters with direct one-click channel launch.
- **Massive Movie, Bangla Natok & Series Library**:
  - Bangla Movies, popular Bangla Natok & Web Series, English, Hindi, Hindi Dubbed, Animation, and a dedicated **Kids Songs** section.
  - High-resolution TMDB posters, ratings, subtitle support, and **Continue Watching** resume queues.
- **Interactive TV Guide (EPG)**:
  - Daily broadcast schedules anchored to **Bangladesh Standard Time (BST / UTC+6)**.
  - Transparent TV Guide overlay accessible directly over live video playback.
- **Optimized for Low-RAM TV Boxes & Variable Networks**:
  - Smooth 60fps UI performance even on 1GB–1.5GB RAM Android TV boxes and Fire TV sticks.
  - Built-in Media3/ExoPlayer engine with **Soft Auto-Reconnect** and expanded live buffers to prevent drops.
- **TV Remote & Mobile Touch Friendly**:
  - 100% D-Pad remote navigable for TVs, plus intuitive touch gestures and seek HUD for mobile & tablet users.
- **Zero-Touch Trakt.tv Integration**:
  - Side-by-side QR code activation from your phone — no typing on a TV remote.
  - Automatic watch history sync, watchlist import, and playback scrobbling.
- **Automated In-App Updater & Backup**:
  - 1-tap update checks and installation directly from **Settings ➔ App Update**, plus local backup & restore.
