# Privacy Policy for Rokon IPTV / PulseTV

**Last updated**: November 19, 2026

Rokon IPTV (PulseTV) ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data when using the PulseTV application.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Storage

- **Local Storage Only**: PulseTV does not collect, track, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to any developer-owned central analytics or advertising servers.
- **User Credentials & Tokens**: All IPTV authentication credentials and Trakt.tv OAuth access tokens are stored securely in local encrypted private app storage on your device.
- **No Third-Party Analytics**: PulseTV contains no third-party advertising SDKs or data brokers.

---

### 2. Trakt.tv Integration

- **Authentication**: When you connect your Trakt account, authentication is performed directly via Trakt's secure OAuth Device Code flow (`https://trakt.tv/activate`).
- **Data Usage**: Your Trakt OAuth tokens are used solely to communicate directly between your device and `api.trakt.tv` to:
  - Scrobble playback progress and mark episodes/movies as watched.
  - Synchronize your personal watch history and favorites.
  - Import your personal watchlists into your device library.
- **Trakt Notice**:
  > **Rokon IPTV uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.**
- For information on how Trakt handles your data, please review the [Trakt Privacy Policy](https://trakt.tv/privacy).

---

### 3. Media Streaming

- PulseTV connects directly to the IPTV server configured by the user or pre-configured pipeline backend to stream live channels and on-demand media.
- Video stream playback requests and telemetry are handled locally on device.
